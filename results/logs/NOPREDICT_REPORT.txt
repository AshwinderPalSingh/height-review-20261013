The same architecture retrained with \texttt{predict\_method\,=\,'none'}---no
trajectory prediction of any kind, occlusion inference retained---for $350$\,M steps
across three seeds reaches $88.07 \pm 0.90\%$ success (Table~\ref{tab:deployable}),
against HEIGHT's $88.03\%$ (per-seed $p=0.21, 0.46, 0.48$). Privileged ground-truth futures were
therefore worth $+0.01$\,pp of success---an upper bound, since the oracle arm also
received ${\sim}426$\,M steps against this arm's $350$\,M.
The per-seed intrusion ratios are $5.112, 5.215, 5.253$ against HEIGHT's $5.658$ (per-seed Welch
$p=9.5\times10^{-4}, 0.007, 0.013$).
HEIGHT fine-tuned to a matched $350$\,M steps reaches $87.77\%$ success and an intrusion ratio of $5.359$; 0 of 3 seeds of the deployable system intrude significantly less than this compute-matched control.
